This case study analyzes the impact of AI-driven recommendation algorithms in social media platforms 
such as Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok. These systems use user data like likes, watch time, and 
interactions to deliver personalized content, helping users manage information overload and improving
engagement.

However, the study highlights major ethical concerns. Algorithms can reinforce bias and discrimination,
violate user privacy through extensive data collection, and lack transparency due to their “black box”
nature. Additionally, they often promote misinformation and emotionally driven content, influencing 
user behavior and decision-making.

The case study also examines societal impacts, including mental health issues, political polarization,
and digital inequality. It emphasizes the risks of over-dependence on AI systems and reduced critical 
thinking.

Overall, the study concludes that while recommendation systems provide benefits, responsible design,
transparency, regulation, and user awareness are essential to ensure ethical and fair use of AI.
