This project presents a conceptual design of an AI-driven system for NASA space missions, focusing on
how Artificial Intelligence can improve mission efficiency, decision-making, and automation in complex
space environments. The project explores the use of AI for tasks such as autonomous navigation, 
real-time data analysis, anomaly detection, and mission planning. 

The system is designed to support astronauts and mission control by processing large volumes of space
data, predicting potential risks, and optimizing operations with minimal human intervention. It 
highlights how AI can enhance safety, reduce human workload, and enable faster responses in critical
situations.

Additionally, the project emphasizes the role of intelligent systems in future space exploration, 
including deep space missions where communication delays make autonomy essential. Overall, this project
demonstrates how AI can play a key role in advancing space technology and exploration.
