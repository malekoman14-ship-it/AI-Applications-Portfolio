This module explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the media and entertainment industry
by enhancing content creation, personalization, and user engagement. AI acts as a creative partner, 
supporting artists, musicians, and writers in generating new ideas, music, designs, and written 
content using technologies like machine learning and generative models.

Key topics include AI in music composition, generative art using GANs, and Natural Language Generation
(NLG) for automated writing. The module also explains how recommendation systems, such as collaborative
and content-based filtering, personalize user experiences on platforms like Netflix and Spotify.

Additionally, it highlights important challenges such as deepfakes, misinformation, privacy concerns, 
and ethical issues related to ownership and accountability. The module emphasizes responsible AI 
development, including transparency, fairness, and regulation, to ensure AI is used ethically while 
continuing to innovate in media and entertainment.
