This case study explores the potential of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) in transforming the 
healthcare industry. Unlike narrow AI, AGI can perform a wide range of cognitive tasks, enabling 
holistic decision-making across clinical, operational, and research domains. The study highlights 
how AGI could address major healthcare challenges such as diagnostic errors, workforce burnout, data 
fragmentation, and limited access to specialized care.

Key proposed applications include a Universal Clinical Reasoning Engine, which integrates patient data
to deliver accurate diagnoses and personalized treatments, and AGI-powered drug discovery systems that
significantly reduce development time. The study also emphasizes AGI’s potential to improve global 
healthcare access by providing expert-level guidance in underserved regions.

However, critical concerns such as data privacy, algorithmic bias, accountability, and workforce 
displacement are discussed. Overall, the case study highlights that while AGI can revolutionize 
healthcare, its success depends on ethical governance, transparency, and responsible implementation.
