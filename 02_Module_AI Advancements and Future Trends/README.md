This module focuses on recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence and explores future trends 
shaping the field. It highlights major breakthroughs such as advanced language models, multimodal 
AI systems, and open-source innovations that improve accessibility and efficiency. These developments
show how AI is becoming more powerful, scalable, and widely usable across industries.

Key topics include improvements in machine learning architectures, integration of text, image, and 
audio processing, and the growing role of reinforcement learning in enhancing applications like 
chatbots and translation systems. The module also explains real-world applications in healthcare, 
finance, and manufacturing, demonstrating AI’s impact on automation and decision-making.

Additionally, it covers ethical, legal, and societal considerations such as bias, transparency, and 
regulation, along with future trends like AI integration across sectors and its impact on jobs. 
Overall, this module provides a clear understanding of current AI progress and its future direction.
