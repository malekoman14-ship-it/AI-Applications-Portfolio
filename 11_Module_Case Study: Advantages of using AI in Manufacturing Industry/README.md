This case study analyzes the impact of Artificial Intelligence in the manufacturing industry, focusing
on Siemens’ Amberg plant and its use of AI-driven predictive maintenance. AI technologies such as 
machine learning, computer vision, IoT, and digital twin systems enable real-time monitoring, fault 
prediction, and automated quality inspection, transforming traditional manufacturing into a data-driven
ecosystem.

The study highlights key benefits, including reduced downtime, lower maintenance costs, improved 
production efficiency, and near-perfect product quality (up to 99.9988%). It also explains how AI shifts
operations from reactive to predictive decision-making.

However, challenges such as high initial investment, data integration issues, workforce reskilling, and
cybersecurity risks are discussed.

Additionally, the case study proposes an AI-based energy optimization system using reinforcement learning
to reduce energy costs and improve sustainability. Overall, it demonstrates how AI enhances efficiency,
innovation, and competitiveness in modern manufacturing.
