This module focuses on how Artificial Intelligence is transforming healthcare and agriculture through
real-world applications. In healthcare, AI is used for diagnostics, medical imaging, personalized 
medicine, and drug discovery, helping improve patient outcomes and reduce costs. Technologies like 
NLP, wearable devices, and robotic surgeries enhance treatment, monitoring, and hospital management. 
AI also supports telemedicine, mental health analysis, and predictive decision-making in clinical 
systems.

In agriculture, AI enables precision farming, crop and soil monitoring, and climate prediction to 
improve productivity and sustainability. Tools such as drones, computer vision, and predictive 
analytics help farmers optimize irrigation, detect diseases, and manage livestock efficiently. 
Automation through agricultural robotics and AI-driven supply chains improves food production.

The module also highlights ethical challenges like data privacy, accessibility, and environmental 
impact, while emphasizing AI’s role in building sustainable and efficient systems for the future.
