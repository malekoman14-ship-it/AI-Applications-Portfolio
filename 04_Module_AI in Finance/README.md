This module focuses on how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the financial industry through 
advanced technologies like Machine Learning, NLP, and Computer Vision. AI improves efficiency, risk 
management, and customer experience by automating processes such as loan approvals, fraud detection,
and financial forecasting.

Key topics include algorithmic trading, where AI analyzes market patterns and executes trades at high 
speed, and fraud detection using real-time monitoring and behavioral analysis. AI also enhances 
credit scoring through alternative data, enables personalized customer service via chatbots, and 
supports portfolio and risk management with predictive analytics.

The module also highlights ethical considerations such as fairness, transparency, and data privacy 
in financial systems.

Assignment

As part of this module, I developed an AI-Based Stock News Sentiment Tracker / Web Crawler, which 
analyzes financial news and extracts sentiment insights to support smarter investment decisions.
