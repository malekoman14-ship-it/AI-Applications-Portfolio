This module explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming urban environments into smart, 
connected, and efficient cities. It explains the shift from traditional transportation systems to 
AI-powered systems that use real-time data, predictive analytics, and automation to improve mobility
and reduce congestion.

Key topics include smart city foundations such as IoT sensors, 5G connectivity, and AI platforms that
enable data-driven decision-making. AI applications like intelligent traffic management, predictive 
traffic analytics, and computer vision improve safety, reduce travel time, and optimize resource usage.
The module also covers connected vehicle technologies (V2X), smart parking systems, and AI-powered 
public transportation that enhance user experience and efficiency.

Additionally, it highlights environmental monitoring, sustainability, and urban logistics, along with
challenges like data privacy and security. Overall, this module demonstrates how AI creates smarter, 
safer, and more sustainable urban transportation systems.
