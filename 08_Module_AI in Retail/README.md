This module explores how Artificial Intelligence is transforming the retail industry by improving 
customer experience, operational efficiency, and business decision-making. AI enables personalized 
shopping through recommendation systems, chatbots, and virtual assistants, while technologies like 
computer vision and NLP enhance interactions and automate processes.

Key topics include personalization engines, virtual and augmented reality for product visualization,
and smart store technologies such as automated checkout and shelf monitoring. The module also covers 
supply chain optimization, demand forecasting, warehouse automation, and store operations like staff
scheduling and energy management.

Additionally, it highlights implementation strategies, key performance metrics, and challenges such 
as data quality, integration, and skill gaps. Ethical considerations including privacy, bias, and 
transparency are also discussed. Overall, this module demonstrates how AI helps retailers deliver 
efficient, data-driven, and customer-focused shopping experiences.
